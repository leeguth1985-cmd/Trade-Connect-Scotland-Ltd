Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Quick answers to the questions businesses are most likely to ask.

Can another company from my trade join the same area?

No. Each local area is limited to one member per trade or professional category. Once your position is secured, another business from the same category would not be accepted into that local group.

What area does my membership cover?

Your membership is linked to the local Trade Connect Scotland area or chapter you join. The exact area boundaries and chapter structure will be confirmed as the network develops.

How often will meetings take place?

The meeting schedule is still being finalised. Dates, times and venue details will be confirmed before launch.

What happens if my category is already taken?

You can still register your interest. We can discuss availability in another local area or keep your details for a future chapter.

Who can join?

Trade Connect Scotland is designed for reliable trades, suppliers, property professionals and other businesses that naturally benefit from referrals and collaboration within the construction and property sectors.

How much is membership?

Membership is £450 per year, equivalent to £37.50 per month. The aim is for the value of referrals, introductions and protected local category access to comfortably outweigh the annual fee.
